Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Multiport ("Multiport," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information across this website and the Multiport products — Multiport Games, Multiport Counter App, and Multiport Support (together, the "Services"). Multiport is built and operated by a single independent developer, not a larger company.

Information we collect

What's collected depends on which Service you use:

  • Account information. Some Services let you create an account, which may include an email address, username, and password. Creating an account is optional unless a specific feature requires it.
  • Usage data. Basic technical information (such as device/browser type and pages visited) may be collected automatically to keep the Services working reliably and to understand how they're used.
  • Content you provide. Information you submit directly, such as messages sent to our support or DMCA email addresses.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Services
  • To create and manage your account, if you choose to have one
  • To respond to support requests and legal notices
  • To detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, or security issues

Cookies and local storage

Individual Services may use cookies or browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, or otherwise make the product work as intended. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect functionality.

How information is shared

Multiport does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only with:

  • Service providers who help operate the Services (for example, hosting infrastructure), under obligations to protect it
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Multiport or others

Data retention

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the Services or as required by law. You can request deletion of your account information at any time — see "Your choices" below.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing support@multiportllc.com.

Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
